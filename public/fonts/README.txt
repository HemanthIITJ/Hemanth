Official Google Fonts CSS:
https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter:wght@400..700&family=Instrument+Serif:ital@0;1&display=swap

Fonts are unchanged Latin-subset WOFF2 files from Google Fonts.
Inter is the normal variable face requested for weights 400-700.
Instrument Serif includes separate normal and italic faces at weight 400.
Use font-display: swap in the site CSS.
